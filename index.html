How an engagement runs

Diagnose it. Plan it. Build it.

Every engagement follows the same three steps, whether it’s a single project or ongoing support.

  1. 01

    Diagnose

    A working session and a close look at your operation, team, and numbers to find what’s really holding growth back.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A practical plan with priorities, owners, timelines, and the measures that show it’s working.

  3. 03

    Build

    Marc works alongside your team to put the plan in place, adjusts along the way, and hands over systems your team can run.

Larger engagements

One accountable lead, plus the right specialists.

For multi-part projects, Nunn Group serves as the prime contractor and brings in vetted specialists, all working from one plan with one point of contact.

About

Meet Marc.

Marc Nunn is a mechanical engineer who spent 25 years in executive roles across engineering, manufacturing, distribution and logistics, project management, supply chain, operations, and human resources.

He has led large cross-functional teams, solved problems that span departments, and delivered projects on tight timelines. He has worked with organizations including Baxter, Corning, Target, Boeing, and UChicago Medicine.

Outside client work, he makes time to give back, especially in STEM and in his community.
