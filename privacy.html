Privacy notice

Last updated November 6, 2026

This notice explains what Nunn Group LLC (“Nunn Group,” “we”) collects through nunngroupllc.com and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Messages you send. When you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, organization (if you give it), and your message.
  • Growth check answers. Your answers stay in your browser. We only receive your scores if you choose “Send Marc my results” and then send the message.
  • Bookings. Calendly runs our booking calendar. To make booking open quickly, Calendly’s booking tool loads in the background a few seconds after you arrive. If you book a call, Calendly collects the details you enter, under Calendly’s own privacy policy.
  • Site visits. If analytics is turned on, Google Analytics records how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed and buttons clicked, using cookies.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, schedule and prepare for calls, provide our services, and improve this website. We don’t sell your information.

Who helps us

We use a few service providers to run the site: Formspree delivers contact form messages, Calendly handles bookings, GitHub hosts the site, and Google Analytics measures visits when it’s turned on. They process information for us under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep it

We keep messages and booking details as long as needed to respond, work with you, and meet our legal and business record-keeping needs.

Your choices

To ask what we have about you, or to have it corrected or deleted, email marc@nunngroupllc.com. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings.

Changes to this notice

If we change this notice, we’ll post the new version here and update the date at the top.
